WELCOME TO THE FIRM

Galane Letsoalo Attorneys.

Representation founded on diligence and commitment.

Founded by Letsoalo Galane in 2018, the practice assists individuals, families and businesses with a broad range of legal matters from its Pretoria-based office.

Our work includes litigation and legal advisory services, with particular consideration given to negotiated settlement and mediation where they are appropriate.

Our doors are open to people from all communities, within South Africa and beyond.
