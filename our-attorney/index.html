Meet Letsoalo Galane.

DIRECTOR · ATTORNEY OF THE HIGH COURT

Letsoalo Galane

LLB · University of Limpopo

Letsoalo Galane founded Galane Letsoalo Attorneys in 2018 after building professional experience in litigation and legal practice, including time as a director at Gibbs Mkhwanazi Galane Inc.

His published profile describes experience in court proceedings and a focus on defending and protecting people's lawful rights. He leads the firm's work across criminal, civil, contractual and other legal matters.

The firm considers alternative dispute resolution alongside litigation, with a view to resolving matters responsibly where negotiation is practicable.
