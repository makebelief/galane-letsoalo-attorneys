CRIMINAL LAW · GUIDE

What to prepare before a criminal-law consultation

Legal consultations are more productive when clients bring charge sheets, notices to appear and a clear chronology of events. Do not delay obtaining urgent advice where liberty or deadlines are involved.

This information is introductory, does not create an attorney–client relationship and is not a substitute for personal legal advice.
