OUR STORY

Representation with purpose.

Galane Letsoalo Attorneys was founded by Mr Letsoalo Galane in 2018, following his professional experience with Gibbs Mkhwanazi Galane Inc. The firm provides legal services across personal, employment and business matters.

Its public profile emphasises diligent court work, protection of constitutional rights and consideration of mediation as a way to avoid unnecessary costs and delays where appropriate.

We aim to help clients make informed decisions about their next legal step while remaining mindful of the seriousness and individuality of each matter.
